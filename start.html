1What each button does

Double-click the sasAI icon on your desktop. This window opens — the numbers match the list below.

1Load Data…Open your measurement file (.dat .abs .txt .xml .h5).
2↻ RestartClear everything and start a new sample.
3Chat · AI Config · 🔑 Keys · ReportChat: what the analysis is doing, and a box for questions. AI Config: settings for the AI, voice and Telegram. Keys: API keys and tokens, kept only on this computer. Report: write and save the report.
4AI:Which AI answers. Built-in is the AI on your own computer. Under it: SAS Model (the model sasAI chose) and chi2 (how closely it fits — near 1 is best).
5PlotYour data (dots) and the fitted model (line). The P(r) tab shows the distance distribution.
6▶ Auto-FitThe main button: sasAI picks a model and fits it for you. >> RUN FIT fits a model you chose yourself, STOP stops. The same row also has Web Search (look up the literature first), Compute/Plot (draw a model without fitting) and Export (save results as CSV, DAT or JSON).
7Model · Fit Options · Polydispersity · Magnetism · Combine ModelsChoose and adjust a model by hand — for experienced users.
8Fit Log · KB · S-EVO · Refs · Display · FlowFit Log: every fitting step. KB: your past fits. S-EVO: sasAI's growing knowledge library. Refs: literature for this sample. Display: font sizes. Flow: the analysis stages, live.
9Report a problemTell the sasAI team what went wrong — your log is attached automatically.
10PicoSAS bubbleYour AI assistant (called 小伐 in Chinese). Click it to chat.
11◢ HolographicThe look of the window: Holographic, Dark or Light.
MenusProject: Save Project… · Open Project… · Recall (re-verify)… Help: Check for Updates · Contribute Knowledge… · Report a Problem…

2Your AI assistant

sasAI comes with its own AI. It runs on your computer — no account, no key, no fee, and it keeps working without internet.

  1. The first time, the AI downloads by itself

    About 5 GB, once — usually 10 to 40 minutes. The bottom-right corner shows the progress. You can keep working; if you close sasAI, it continues next time.

  2. Open the assistant

    Click the round picture at the bottom-right of your screen. Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + A. It can answer once the download above has finished.

  3. Ask anything

    Type your question and press Enter, or tap a suggestion. It answers in the language you write in.

    1SuggestionsTap one to ask it.
    2Question box · ➤Type, then Enter or ➤.
    3🎤 MicrophoneTalk instead of typing (chapter 6).
    4🔊 SpeakerRead the answers aloud.
    5EN / 中Switch the answer language.
    🎧 · — · ■🎧: you can interrupt it by talking. —: shrink back to the round picture. ■ (while it answers): stop.

  4. “AI:” says Built-in — that's the offline AI

    It is already selected. Built-in answers everything — chat, analysis and reports — on your own computer.

3Change the AI model

Optional — the built-in AI is enough to start. Choose A, B or C.

A. Another built-in model (offline)

  1. Open AI Config → Built-in LLM

    Click the AI Config tab on the left. Built-in LLM is the first box.

  2. Pick a model 1, click Download 2

    Or type a name and click Search HF to find any model on HuggingFace. When the download ends, sasAI asks “Switch the AI provider to 'Built-in' now?” — click Yes.

  3. Switch between models you already have 3 4

    Pick one under Installed and click Use — no download. Bigger models are smarter but slower and need more memory: an 8B model needs about 12 GB of RAM.

B. A cloud AI — Gemini, Claude or MiniMax

  1. Get an API key

    For Google Gemini, follow chapter 4. Claude: console.anthropic.com. MiniMax: platform.minimaxi.com.

  2. Paste it in the 🔑 Keys tab, click Save

    Pictures in chapter 4, steps 4–5.

  3. Choose it in the “AI:” box

    Pick Gemini, Claude or MiniMax. The quick chat stays on the built-in AI; the cloud AI takes the analysis and the reports. What you send goes to that company.

C. Your own AI server (LM Studio, Ollama)

AI Config → Edge AI: type a Name, the Endpoint (for example http://localhost:1234/v1), click ↻ to list its models, choose one, click Test, then Save AI Config. Finally choose Edge AI in the “AI:” box.

4Get a Google (Gemini) API key

Needed only if you want Google's Gemini as your AI. You need a Google account.

  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in

    Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account. Accept the terms the first time.

  2. Create the key

    New users get a key automatically. Otherwise click Create API key.

    Simplified drawing — Google's page may look a little different.

  3. Copy the key

    Click the copy button next to the key. Treat it like a password: never post it or email it.

  4. Paste it in sasAI: 🔑 Keys → Gemini API key → Save

    In sasAI, open the 🔑 Keys tab. Under AI providers, paste into Gemini API key and click Save. The label changes from empty to saved.

  5. Choose Gemini in the “AI:” box

    Done. To pick a Gemini version: AI Config → Cloud AI → Gemini Model (gemini-2.5-flash is fast).

Before you send unpublished data

With a free Gemini key, Google may use what you send to improve its products, and asks users not to send sensitive or confidential information. For confidential samples, stay on Built-in.

If your key leaks, delete it in AI Studio and create a new one.

5Telegram: use sasAI from your phone

Chat with sasAI, send it a data file and get the plots and the PDF report — from your phone. sasAI must stay open on your computer.

  1. Create your bot with @BotFather

    In Telegram, search @BotFather, open it and send /newbot. Give any name (for example My sasAI), then a username that ends in bot — 5 to 32 letters, numbers or _ (for example my_sasai_bot; it cannot be changed later).

    Simplified drawing — BotFather's wording may differ.

  2. Copy the token

    BotFather's last message has a token that looks like 123456789:AA…. Copy it. It works like a password for your bot.

  3. Paste it in sasAI: 🔑 Keys → Telegram bot token → Save

    Open the 🔑 Keys tab, click Remote control (Telegram) to open it, paste into Telegram bot token, click Save.

  4. Turn the bot on

    AI Config tab → click the title Remote Control (Telegram) to open it → click Enable bot 1. It says “✅ Bot enabled — starting on this machine.”

  5. Message your bot to get your chat ID

    In Telegram open your bot (t.me/my_sasai_bot, with your bot's name) and tap Start. It answers with your chat ID — a number. Copy it.

  6. Paste the chat ID, click Save chat IDs

    Back in sasAI: paste the number into Allowed chat IDs 2 and click Save chat IDs 3 (picture at step 4). It says “✅ @your_bot running — locked to …”. Now only you can use this bot.

  7. Use it

    Send your data file as a file (📎 → File). Then type analyze. Later ask send the plot or PDF report. You can also just chat.

    If the token ever leaks: send /token to @BotFather for a new one, then paste it in 🔑 Keys again.

6Voice: talk to it, and record your voice Windows

Speak your questions and hear the answers. In this beta, voice works on Windows; on macOS and Linux, type instead.

  1. Click the microphone and talk

    In the chat window, click 🎤. It turns cyan while it listens — just talk, nothing to hold. Click it again to stop. The very first time it may need a minute to get ready.

  2. Start with its name

    Say “PicoSAS, …” (in Chinese: “小伐，…”). Until it knows your voice (next step), it only follows sentences that start with its name — so people talking nearby don't set it off.

  3. Record your voice — 6 seconds, once

    AI Config tab → Voice box → click Enroll voiceprint (6s) 1. (Or just say or type “PicoSAS, enroll my voice”.)

  4. Press OK and read the sentence shown

    Recording starts when you press OK and lasts 6 seconds. Read in your normal voice, at your usual distance from the computer.

  5. Done: “Enrolled ✓”

    The Voice box now says “Enrolled ✓ — hands-free accepts your voice (or the assistant's name).” It listens to you — no name needed. Your voiceprint stays on this computer. Record again any time; Clear deletes it.

  6. Hear the answers

    Click 🔊 at the top of the chat window. You can interrupt it just by talking. English is spoken offline; the Chinese voice needs the internet.

If it doesn't hear you well

  • Wrong microphone? Voice box → Microphone 2 → pick yours.
  • Nothing at all? Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone: turn on Microphone access and Let desktop apps access your microphone.
  • Short Chinese sentences written as English? Voice language 3 → 中文.
  • Want more accurate recognition? STT model 4 → medium (one-time ~1.5 GB download, a bit slower).

7From your data to a report

The whole analysis in eight steps.

  1. Load your data

    Click Load Data… and choose your file. Your curve appears on the plot.

  2. Leave Web Search on

    For a sample sasAI doesn't know yet, Web Search looks up the literature first. Turn it off to go straight to fitting.

  3. Click ▶ Auto-Fit and describe your sample

    Write one line: what it is, the solvent, SAXS or SANS, and what you want to know. Not sure? Leave it empty — the data will speak. Click ✓ Use this.

  4. Wait a few minutes

    The Flow tab on the right shows each stage as it runs. STOP cancels.

  5. Read the result

    The line on the plot is the fitted model. SAS Model shows which model sasAI chose and chi2 how closely it follows your data (near 1 is best). The Chat tab explains the answer — ask PicoSAS “Why this model?” if you want to know more.

  6. Report tab → Generate

    Open the Report tab on the left. If you like, type your measurement conditions in Exp. conditions — anything you leave out is written as “not specified”, never made up. Click Generate.

  7. Save the report: PDF or DOCX

    When the report appears, click PDF 3 (or DOCX for Word) and choose where to save it.

  8. Keep your work

    Project → Save Project… saves the whole analysis; Open Project… brings it back. Export saves the numbers as CSV, DAT or JSON.

Stuck?

Click Report a problem at the bottom-right of the sasAI window (or Help → Report a Problem…). Describe what happened; sasAI adds its log and shows you everything before it is sent.
